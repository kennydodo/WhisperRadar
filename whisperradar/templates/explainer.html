How it works

  • Scenes anchor to subtitles.srt cue numbers (stage 4). Cue ranges must not overlap; gaps are held by the earlier scene.
  • Objects: text, label, path, bar, counter, grid, blocks, callout, image. Actions: fade_in, fade_out, slide, type_on, draw, count, grow, reveal, emphasis.
  • Coordinates are normalized (0..1 of the canvas). Timing is integer frames at the production fps; narration stays the master clock.
  • Rendered scenes are cached in out\explainer (content-hashed); re-renders reuse unchanged scenes.
